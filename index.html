The Study Block

One class, one block, one key statement. Set the timer, mark with a purpose, ask the questions that uncover the burden, and capture what you saw before it fades.

        Patterns to watch for in the ministry

        • “Not… but…”He is correcting a natural idea. The point is on the “but” side.
        • “This means” · “this is why” · “therefore”These often introduce the point right after the verses.
        • A phrase that keeps coming backRepetition across several pages usually signals the burden.
        Quick check: if more than about a third of a page is marked, you’re highlighting what’s interesting, not what’s main.

        Ask your way to the burden

        The basic question under all the others:

        How do I see this in the Word of God?

        Before reading

        Step 4 · pick three as guiding questions

        • What is the title telling me this message is about?
        • In class, what was the brother impressing on us, not only what he said but why he said it?
        • Which part of this topic is least familiar to me?
        • What would I need to see to explain this lesson to someone else?

        After reading

        Step 6 · the training’s four review questions

        1. What are the main points of this reading, and what are their biblical bases?
        2. How do the main points relate to one another?
        3. What was the burden of this lesson?
        4. What portion of this message enlightened or touched me the most?

        While reading

        Step 5 · ask of each main point

        Biblical basis
        Where is this in the Word? Drive down from the ministry to the verse: what do Paul, John, or Revelation say?
        Gaps
        Is there a jump here? What steps lead from the verse to what Brother Lee saw? Consider it as the Bereans did, not in a doubting way.
        Crucial or secondary
        Does this point touch God’s heart’s desire and His economy, or is it just the easiest part to understand?
        Pace
        Is this point touching me so much that I’ll miss the central burden? Mark it ? and keep moving.
        Prayer
        Can I turn this sentence into a short prayer right now?

        Crystallizing

        Step 7

        • What one sentence holds everything the Lord showed me in this block?
        • What word or title would bring this whole study back when I see it again?
        • What question would lead someone from point A to point B? For example: how can the only begotten Son of God be the Firstborn Son if there is no change in God (Heb. 13:8)?

        Bird’s-eye view

        Step 8

        • How does this week’s key statement connect to the earlier weeks?
        • Where does this sit in God’s economy: the Triune God, the Spirit for our experience, the experience of life, the New Jerusalem?

        Capture it before it fades

        Light shines and then flees. End every block by compressing what you saw, and keep it where you can find it again.

        Whittle it down

        Three sentencesEverything the Lord showed you in this block
        One sentenceYour key statement for the week
        One word or titleThe hook that brings it all back

        When you return to the title later, the whole study comes back with it. You don’t have to redo the process.

        From light to speaking

        RevelationLight from the Lord in prayer. It fades quickly.
        ThoughtsCapture it with your mind and write it down right away.
        Inner wordsA word or sentence taught by the Spirit (1 Cor. 2:13). This is the burden.
        Outer wordsRelease the burden by speaking, with words gathered from the Bible and the ministry.
        A burden, but no wordsSpend more time in the Word. You need more outer words.
        Words, but no burdenSpend more time with the Lord in prayer.

        Ground rules

        • Gather before the clock starts.Syllabus, outline, reading assignment, Recovery Version with footnotes, class notes, pen, highlighters, and this class’s key statement log.
        • Keep strictly to your schedule.Study extra for a class only after every class has had its hour.
        • Don’t stall on one verse.If a point touches you, mark it ? and come back, so you still finish and still catch the central burden.
        • Balance prayer and the word, about 50/50.Concentrated prayer at the start and end of the block, short prayers sprinkled through the reading.
        • Never cut the produce step.If time runs short, trim the reading, not the writing and speaking.
        • Speak it to someone.Explaining the lesson to another person is the most active way to study. Lesson summaries are due for two classes a week; write each the same day as its class.
