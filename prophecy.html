Preparing a Prophecy

To prophesy is to speak for Christ, speak forth Christ, and speak Christ into others for the building up of the church.

Ingredients During the week Compose Practice In the meeting Checklist

Three ingredients

A prophecy needs all three.

The Word

The content. Without the Word you may be speaking, but not prophesying. Speak mainly what you have seen in the truth.

The Spirit

The present inspiration. Live and walk by the Spirit, and stay open to Him while you speak.

Vision

Light on God’s economy, the church, the saints, and the present need. It comes from the Lord in the light, not from analyzing.

Every believer can prophesy, and should. Don’t hold back because you feel short in experience. If you speak the truth faithfully, the Lord can bring you into its reality.

During the week

A prophecy comes from labor on Christ through the week, not from hoping something comes to you in the meeting.

Your harvest
Gather the week’s notes. Now you don’t have to wonder what to speak.
  • Each morning, enjoy the Lord in the Word.Write one short note of what touched you, what you saw, or what you enjoyed.
  • Don’t be picky with the Word.Take what the Lord gives you and feed on it.
  • Muse on it.Pray it back, turn it over, and carry it through the day. Don’t pass on food you haven’t digested.
  • Use the ministry helps.The footnotes and the Life-studies open the verses for you.

Come to the Lord fresh. You can’t rely on yesterday’s revelation, even for the same words. Prayer comes first, then the speaking.

Time is estimated at about 130 words a minute. Keep one main point with supporting points; don’t summarize the whole message. Writing it all out is for preparing, not for reading in the meeting.

Practice it

Speak it again and again before the meeting.

To yourself, out loud→Again and again→To one or two people→Ask for fellowship if the Lord leads

Listening perfects you too. Notice how others open a verse and feed the saints. And if a classmate shares the same point, share it anyway. You will supply the Body in your own way.

In the meeting

Before you stand, pray yourself into the burden. Don’t let other things occupy you that day.

Do

  • Speak Christ: what He is, what He has done, what you have enjoyed of Him.
  • Exercise your spirit, your voice, and your whole being.
  • Speak clearly, loud enough to be heard, and with life.
  • Stay open to fresh light while you speak.
  • Follow the flow of the meeting. Two to three minutes, or as arranged.

Avoid

  • Reading from your phone or your notes.
  • Long stories, or yourself as the center.
  • Trying to cover everything you know.
  • Negative speaking, or indirectly correcting others.
  • Complicated illustrations and unrelated subjects.

The test of a burden: after you release it, you feel lighter, and something living has been deposited in others.
