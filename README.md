# Studying the Ministry

A small study companion site for class study, built from the training's study guidelines and study fellowship notes.

- **Study Block** (`index.html`): a 60- or 90-minute study timer that walks through each step, a marking key for highlighting and annotating, a question bank for finding the burden, and ways to capture what you saw.
- **Class Notes** (`class-notes.html`): how to set up a note page, what to listen for, the five minutes after class, and turning notes into understanding later that day.
- **Outlines** (`outlines.html`): finding the governing thought, following the flow of the Roman numerals, connecting points to verses, and sorting by importance.
- **Prophecy** (`prophecy.html`): the three ingredients of a prophecy, a builder with a three-minute estimate, practice, delivery, and a checklist.
- **Lord’s Day** (`lords-day.html`): the week from morning revival to the Lord’s Day prophesying meeting: looking ahead at the outline, each morning’s portion with a six-day notes tracker (synced across devices through a private GitHub gist, or by copying a sync code), gathering and composing on Saturday night, preparing on the Lord’s Day morning, the meeting itself, and a checklist.

## Use it

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step or install needed.
