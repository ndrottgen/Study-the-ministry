Lord’s Day Prophesying

The prophesying meeting is the harvest of a week with the Lord. Enjoy Him each morning, gather what you received, and come to the meeting with something to speak for the building up of the church.

The week in four steps

A prophecy comes from labor on Christ through the week, not from hoping something comes to you in the meeting.

1Lord’s Day afternoon

Look ahead

Make a quick pass through the coming week’s outline to see what the week is about.

2Mon–Sat mornings

Enjoy and note

Pray over a small portion, then write one short note of what you received.

3Saturday night

Gather and compose

Put the six notes together, compose one short prophecy, and practice it at home.

4Lord’s Day

Prepare and bring it

Pray yourself ready at home, then come to the meeting with something to offer.

Like bringing produce to the feast. Israel labored on the good land and never came to the feasts empty-handed. We labor on Christ through the week and bring Him to the meeting, to offer to God and enjoy with the saints.

Behind the routine is a living. Be revived every morning, overcome every day, deal with sins as they come up, and stay filled with the Spirit. Without that, even true light becomes empty talk.

Each morning

Enjoy the Lord in a small portion of the Word, then write down what you received.

  • Keep the portion small.Ten verses or fewer is enough. Concentrate on the two or three that touch you.
  • Pray, study, recite, prophesy.Pray over the portion to touch your spirit, connect its points to verses, say back the line that caught you, and practice speaking it.
  • Don’t be picky with the Word.Take what the Lord gives you and feed on it.
  • Use the ministry helps.The footnotes and the Life-studies open the verses for you.
  • Muse on it.Pray it back, turn it over, and carry it through the day. Don’t pass on food you haven’t digested.
  • Speak it during the week.Tell the Lord what you enjoyed, and share it with a saint. Speaking it is part of how it becomes yours.
  • Write one short note.What touched you, what you saw, or what you enjoyed. A few words are enough to remind you.

Come to the Lord fresh. You can’t rely on yesterday’s revelation, even for the same words. Prayer comes first, then the speaking.

Saturday night

Gather the harvest. Now you don’t have to wonder what to speak.

  • Read the six notes together.Look for what runs through them. The week’s outline gives you a bird’s-eye view.
  • Find the burden.Ask what the Lord has been speaking to you, and to the saints, this week. Then pray according to it.
  • Compose one main point.Under three minutes, standing on a verse. Use the builder on the Prophecy page.
  • Practice it at home.Speak it out loud and check the length. See Practice.

The Lord gives the light and the burden. Your part is to find words that fit it. That labor is how we learn to prophesy.

Lord’s Day morning

Prepare your whole being for the meeting, not only your notes.

  • Pray five to ten minutes before you go.Confess anything between you and the Lord and enjoy His filling. Don’t pray so long that your spirit is tired before the meeting starts.
  • Guard the morning.Don’t let other things occupy you that day, even spiritual things. Avoid friction with anyone, and hold off on calls and mail.
  • Start the meeting at home.Call on the Lord, pray, sing, and praise on the way.
  • Go to speak.Come with the decision to function, like an athlete ready to play, not a spectator.

In the prophesying meeting

The meeting is a feast of Christ with many dishes. It doesn’t need a set subject.

  • The goal is to exhibit Christ.The purpose is to build up the saints for the building up of the Body. Bring what enlightens, nourishes, and builds up.
  • Care for the order.If your portion fits later, wait for that time. When many speak in a good order, the meeting is like one composed message.
  • Leave room for others.Wait for others to finish, and keep to three minutes so that many can speak (1 Cor. 14:31).
  • You still came having something.Even if there’s no time for you to speak, you were exercised and ready (1 Cor. 14:26).

For how to speak once you stand up, see In the meeting on the Prophecy page.
